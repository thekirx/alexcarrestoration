About the shop

A working classic-vehicle shop in Quezon City. Restoration, paint, detailing and ceramic coating, done in one place by the same people.

Who we are

Enthusiasts first

Alex Car Restoration works on classic cars and motorcycles — Mercedes, Datsun, Toyota, Porsche, Morgan, BMW, Vespa — and has been part of the local car show scene for decades.

Everything happens in our own shop: strip-down, bodywork, oven-baked paint, reassembly, detailing and coating. Nothing important gets sent out and quietly returned.

We talk to owners the way enthusiasts talk to each other. Exact model, exact work, honest scope.

How we work

Three things we hold to

01

One shop

Restoration, paint, detailing and coating under the same roof, so the standard does not change halfway through your car.

02

Documented work

We photograph builds as they progress. What you are shown is your car, not a stock image of a similar one.

03

Honest scope

If a car does not need a frame-off restoration, we will say so. If it does, we will explain why before you commit.
