Services

Restoration, paint, detailing and ceramic coating. Four service lines, all carried out in our own shop in Quezon City.

01 — Full restoration

Bare-shell and targeted restoration

Classic cars and motorcycles, taken apart to the level the vehicle actually needs. Bare-shell when every system and surface must be assessed; targeted when the sound parts of the car should remain undisturbed.

Completed builds include the Datsun 240Z, Mercedes-Benz 280SL, Toyota Land Cruiser, Vespa 50S and BMW R50.

See finished builds → Discuss your project

02 — Body & paint

Precision bodywork and oven-baked paint

Panel and surface repair, straightening, preparation and refinishing. Painting is oven-baked in our own booth, which is what makes the finish consistent rather than dependent on the weather.

Panel repaint work can be quoted on its own — you do not need a full restoration to have paint corrected properly.

Ask about paint work →

03 — Detailing & paint correction

Interior and exterior detailing

Paint correction, interior and exterior detailing, and finishing work on both our own restorations and cars brought in for detailing alone.

This is the same standard we apply before a car goes to a show, applied to daily and weekend cars.

Book detailing →

04 — Ceramic coating & protection

Coating, with the prep that makes it worth doing

Ceramic coating applied to finished restorations and to modern cars — including Land Rovers, Porsches and Land Cruisers brought to the shop.

PPF removal is handled here too. Old film comes off and the paint underneath is corrected before anything new goes on.

How we restore

The stages every build goes through

You will see each stage as it happens. We photograph the work in progress, not only the finished car.

01

Assessment

We look at the car, agree the scope, and tell you what the work realistically involves.

02

Strip-down

Disassembly to the level the build requires, so nothing gets finished over a problem.

03

Body & paint

Repair, straightening, preparation and oven-baked refinishing in our booth.

04

Reassembly

The car goes back together, with trim, chrome and detail parts treated as part of the job.

05

Finish & protect

Paint correction, detailing and ceramic coating, then a final inspection before handover.
