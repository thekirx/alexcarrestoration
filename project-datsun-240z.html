Frame-off restoration · Ceramic coating

Datsun 240Z

A full frame-off restoration finished in white, ceramic coated, and photographed in the shop where it was built.

The build

The car came apart completely. A frame-off restoration means the body comes off the chassis, so nothing is finished around a problem — everything underneath is dealt with before a single panel goes back on.

Bodywork and refinishing were done in-house, then corrected and ceramic coated so the finish holds up under show lighting and on the road afterwards.

It was photographed here in the shop, alongside the other cars in progress, because that is where it was built.
