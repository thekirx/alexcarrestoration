Datsun 240Z

A complete bare-shell restoration finished in white, ceramic coated, and photographed in the shop where it was built.

The build

From bare shell to show floor

The 240Z was stripped so its unitary body, panels, trim and mechanical components could be assessed as separate parts of one build. That language matters: this is a bare-shell restoration, not a body lifted from a separate frame.

Bodywork and refinishing were handled in-house, then the paint was corrected and ceramic coated for the finished result photographed in the workshop.

The launch case study can pair each stage with the shop's own progress photographs, repair notes and parts record, giving an owner evidence of the work beneath the finish.
