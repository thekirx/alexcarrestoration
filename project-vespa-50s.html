Vespa 50S

A complete scooter restoration rebuilt and refinished down to the grips, badges, selector and spare wheel.

The build

Small vehicles hide nothing. On a scooter every surface is visible from every angle, so a complete strip-down is the honest way to assess and finish one.

It was stripped completely, repaired, refinished, and reassembled with the details treated as part of the job rather than an afterthought — the leather grip, the selector, the badge, the spare wheel carrier.

It sits in front of the trophy cabinet in the shop, which is where most of our finished work gets photographed.
