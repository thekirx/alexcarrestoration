Mercedes-Benz 280SL

A 1968 280SL taken through precision bodywork, oven-baked painting, and full interior and exterior detailing.

The build

The restoration focused on the body, surface preparation and finish. Surfaces have to be straight before any colour goes near them, and the exact work record can be paired with the shop's progress photography before launch.

The car was painted in our own booth and oven-baked, then detailed inside and out before it left the shop.

The SL is one of the models we are asked about most often, and it is the kind of car where the finish is judged from a metre away.
